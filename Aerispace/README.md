# Aerispace - The Ultimate Focus Engine 🚀

Aerispace is a minimalist, clutter-free Pomodoro timer designed to maximize productivity and deep work. Built with a sleek glassmorphism UI, it features customizable focus sessions, ambient soundscapes, and local session tracking to help you stay in the zone.

## ✨ Features

*   **Clean, Distraction-Free UI:** Beautiful light and dark modes with fluid animations and a modern glassmorphism aesthetic.
*   **Customizable Pomodoro Timer:** Easily adjust durations for your Focus sessions, Short Breaks, and Long Breaks directly from the settings.
*   **Ambient Sound Engine:** Integrated background audio (Rain, Cafe, Fire, Brown Noise) to drown out distractions and boost concentration.
*   **Local Stats Tracking:** Automatically logs your sessions and tracks your lifetime focus hours, daily minutes, and monthly statistics.
*   **Exportable Data:** Export your session history to a CSV file with a single click.
*   **Haptic & Audio Feedback:** Gentle chimes and device vibrations (on supported devices) notify you when a session ends.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

*   **Frontend:** HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript
*   **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (via CDN)
*   **Icons:** Lucide Icons
*   **Audio Handling:** Howler.js
